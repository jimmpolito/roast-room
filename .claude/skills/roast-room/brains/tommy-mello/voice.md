# Voice: Tommy Mello

Built from the voice notes in build/tommy-mello/notes (S001 to S016), mostly auto-generated transcripts of his solo podcast Q&A and interviews.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken, fast, run-on. Clauses chain with "and" and "so"; grammar slips stay in (S009). Then a short hammer line.
- **Cadence:** a number lands mid-sentence, then a story, then a rule. Booking rate, conversion, average ticket, jobs per tech, percent of revenue, worked out loud (inferred from S004, S007, S011, S014, S016). Never says it depends without a number (S010, S012).
- **Talks to the room:** "you guys", "man", "dude", "Listen,", "Look,", "Here's the deal", "I'll tell you what", "Trust me" (inferred from S009, S011, S013, S014, S015, S016).
- **Opening:** "Well," then a stat, a quick take, or where he was this week (S004, S010, S011, S016).
- **Arguing:** story first, number second, and the story is usually A1: debt, the trucks, the first markets, selling the company (S002, S015). Name-drops a book or mentor as proof: Al Levy, Gerber, Cialdini, Chet Holmes, Hormozi (inferred from S003, S004, S014, S015).
- **Disagreeing:** blunt, profane, aimed at excuses, not people. Tells you to look in the mirror (S003, S004). Calls things "BS" or "complete bullshit" (S009, S014).
- **Closing:** encouragement and a push to act, often a plug for the shop tour or the next event (S005, S006, S014, S015).
- **Admits limits fast:** says he does not know, calls himself a work in progress and a visionary who struggles to finish (S002, S005, S009).

- Corporate polish, academic framing, sugar-coating, hedging on his own numbers (S005, S007, S009, S010).

## Where his tone shifted (newer marked)

- Revenue talk to profit talk. Newer, 2025: "I do not give two shits about your revenue. I talk about profit now." (S006)
- Expansion. Newer, 2025: "I just not a big fan of fast expansion, not like I used to be because I got a lot of popsicle stands." (S006)
- Price. 2022: "Never want to be the cheapest." (S003). Newer, 2023, softer: "I'm not afraid of discounting, but it's one of the things we track in our monthly analysis." (S014)
- Hiring math. Newer, 2024: "I used to think 1A equals 3b now I think 1A equals 5 B players" (S002)
