# Principles: Tommy Mello

Built from the four batches of notes on S001 to S016 (his own Q&A podcast episodes 2021 to 2026, plus interviews). 25 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Never be the cheapest; anyone who competes on price commoditizes the trade.** "Never want to be the cheapest." (S003) "Anybody that competes on price is commoditizing their industry." (S011) Sources: 9 (S003, S005, S007, S008, S009, S011, S012, S013, S015). Confidence: high. Nuance: a new company with no reviews cannot start at the top price (S013), and in smaller markets he says he enters as the price leader (S011, 2021).

2. **Profit, not revenue, is the score, and net profit never goes below 10%.** "I do not give two shits about your revenue. I talk about profit now." (S006) "My general rule of thumb is never make less than 10%." (S012) Sources: 7 (S004, S006, S007, S010, S012, S015, S003). Confidence: high. His goals moved from revenue to profit over time (S010, 2023). Inconsistency: S009 (2024) accepts break-even while taking share.

3. **Thin profit means you are priced wrong; raise prices and let the worst customers go.** "The reason you're not going to find profit is because you're not priced right." (S010) "You'll lose 20% of your accounts, but you're losing the stupid ones." (S012) Sources: 6 (S005, S007, S008, S010, S012, S013). Confidence: high.

4. **Fix booking, conversion, and ticket before buying more leads or raising price.** "Buying leads is the easy thing to do." (S004) "don't raise your prices if everything else is broken and expect to make more money until you're booking." (S010) Sources: 6 (S004, S009, S010, S014, S016, S006). Confidence: high.

5. **Judge marketing by the revenue it produces; attribute every dollar.** "I always say, look, I got to see the revenue." (S016) "Attribution is everything in marketing, knowing what's working, what's not." (S009) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S006, S009, S013, S014, S016). Confidence: high.

6. **Whoever can pay the most per lead wins, so build the price to afford it.** "he who pays more per lead will always win" (S004) "I'm charging the customer the cost of the marketing or I'll go broke." (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S016). Confidence: high.

7. **Pay for performance on what each person controls, never flat hourly for speed and quality.** "I hate the word commission. I use the word performance pay." (S004) "Performance pay was a miracle." (S010) Sources: 9 (S003, S004, S007, S008, S009, S010, S013, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

8. **Hire only A players and remove the rest fast.** "You can't afford shitty people." (S008) "I don't believe in performance improvement plans." (S007) Sources: 7 (S002, S005, S006, S007, S008, S012, S014). Confidence: high.

9. **Focus on one trade and one market; side hustles dilute results.** "If you have diluted focus, you get diluted results." (S009) "race horses wear blinders" (S015) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S007, S009, S012, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

10. **Take share in your current market before you open another.** "You better have a lot of market share before you expand" (S006) "Take market share, lots of it before you expand." (S014) Sources: 7 (S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S012, S014). Confidence: high. Changed view: once fast expansion, now cautious (S006, 2025-01-24, newer).

11. **Systems before people, and every system needs a check.** "Build a business around systems. The right systems will get the right people." (S010) "Because systems only work when you have checks and balances." (S006) Sources: 5 (S004, S006, S010, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

12. **Get the score before you act; data comes before training or spending.** "The first thing people need is systems to see the score." (S004) "Get the data first. You can't train on something you don't know where the winners are." (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S013, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

13. **Give key people a stake in the value they create, with a target and a payout you honor.** "And I don't want to make people rich. I want to make them wealthy." (S003) "you better have a target." (S015) Sources: 6 (S003, S006, S008, S010, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

14. **Get the owner out of the truck; the business must run without you.** "you got to get out of the truck." (S004) "And this company should run without me." (S003) Sources: 7 (S003, S004, S007, S010, S011, S013, S015). Confidence: high.

15. **Test small, measure the return, then scale.** "I'd put 50 in and see how much I get back out." (S007) "If I put a dollar in to get a buck 25 out i'm winning" (S006) Sources: 5 (S003, S006, S007, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

16. **Sell options and the experience, not one price.** "If you don't have options, you're kind of screwed." (S014) "People buy the experience. They remember how you made them feel." (S004) Sources: 6 (S004, S007, S010, S013, S014, S011). Confidence: high.

17. **People pay for speed; win on fast and quality, not cheap.** "people will pay for speed over cost" (S003) "Fast pays more money." (S003) Sources: 4 (S003, S006, S015, S016). Confidence: high.

18. **Pick your customers; not every homeowner is your client.** "Not everybody with a garage door is my client." (S006) "instead of going after every client, go after the clients that you know will pay the bills and allow you to make a profit." (S014) Sources: 5 (S003, S006, S008, S014, S016). Confidence: high.

19. **Do not quit marketing in a downturn; cut people, stay lean, keep attributable spend.** "You got to not quit on marketing." (S004) "But right now you're going to have to make some cuts and it's not marketing." (S010) Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S007, S010). Confidence: high.

20. **Brand lowers the cost of everything else.** "Everything does better when you invest into the brand." (S008) Sources: 5 (S003, S004, S008, S010, S016). Confidence: high. Brand raised booking, conversion, and ticket at A1 (S016).

21. **Use W-2 employees, not 1099s, to keep control.** "You definitely, definitely, definitely want to be W-2. You want control of your business." (S005) Sources: 4 (S003, S005, S008, S013). Confidence: high. He allows 1099s when cash is tight (S003).

22. **Borrow small, for assets, and set up credit before you need it.** "If you're not making 10%, do not invest in growth." (S007) "don't borrow money when you need it. Get a delay draw term loan set up when you don't need it." (S010) Sources: 4 (S003, S006, S007, S010). Confidence: high. Changed view: in 2022 he said "I'd say go out there and get a $300,000 loan and blow the company up." (S003, 2022-01-14); in 2024 (newer) "If I was under 10 million, I would never, ever, ever take out a loan" (S007, 2024-02-16).

23. **Do not take work outside your lane just because you can.** "just because you could doesn't mean you should" (S014) Sources: 4 (S007, S012, S014, S015). Confidence: high.

24. **Build to sell; think about enterprise value, not this year's income.** "Not how do I make more money this year. It should be how do I make the company worth more money?" (S013) "build a business to sell it period" (S007) Sources: 5 (S007, S008, S009, S013, S015). Confidence: high.

25. **When the numbers say a market is losing, close it now; belief in a turnaround is the trap.** "because I believed that I could turn those around, just like every entrepreneur does" (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S015). Confidence: medium.
