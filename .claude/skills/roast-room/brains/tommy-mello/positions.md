## Pricing
- **Stance:** Never be the cheapest. Back into price from the gross margin you need and the cost of the whole business, then charge the highest price you can while delivering the best service. Thin profit means you are priced wrong. Sources: S003, S005, S007, S008, S010, S011, S012, S013, S015, S016.
- 2022-01: "You need to back up into your gross margin, and that's how you should do your pricing. A guy like you, I'd say 65%, 70% gross margin is what I'd want." (S003) "Never want to be the cheapest." (S003)
- 2023-05: "The reason you're not going to find profit is because you're not priced right." (S010) But fix booking and conversion first: "don't raise your prices if everything else is broken and expect to make more money until you're booking." (S010)
- 2024-12: "I don't think you're charging the right prices if you're barely profitable." (S012) On a 10% raise: "You'll lose 20% of your accounts, but you're losing the stupid ones." (S012)
- 2026-10: "most home service companies are weak on marketing. They're not priced correctly." (S016)
- **Exception for a new name in a market:** 2021-10: "I go in as a price leader. I really do." (S011) 2025-02 (newer): "you can't start out as the highest price." (S013) Earn the right with reviews and relationships first.
- **Read:** owners undercharge out of fear: "You can't because you're still not priced right because you're scared." (S008, 2023-08)

## Competing on price and differentiation
- **Stance:** Do not let the customer compare like for like. Change the product (trademarked parts, longer-cycle springs, better warranty, speed) so the quote is oranges against apples. Sources: S003, S011, S013, S015, S016.
- 2021-10: "Anybody that competes on price is commoditizing their industry." (S011) "I don't sell apples to apples when I sell oranges." (S011)
- 2022-01: "when you sell stuff sell stuff no one else has" (S003) "the competitors they are copying are dead broke. So why on earth are you copying them?" (S003)
- 2026-02: pick two of quality, speed, price: "You pick two out of the three years that you get. You'll never be all three." (S015) A1 plays quality and speed.
- 2026-10: same rule, never on cheapest, win on fast with after-hours capacity (S016). Price transparency commoditizes; "home service not the same as products?" (S016)

## Booking rate and the call center
- **Stance:** The call center is the first place he looks for money. Target about 85 to 91% booked; every point is real money; staff the phones because a missed call costs more than a CSR. Sources: S004, S005, S006, S009, S010, S013, S014, S015, S016.
- 2023-03: "You should be a 90% booking rate. If you're over 90, you're probably booking some calls that might not be worth it." (S004)
- 2023-12: "booking rates are where I see the biggest opportunity for every business I look at." (S014) Benchmarks: "So booking rate should be good company, 75 and above, great company, 85 and above." (S014)
- 2025-01: "if I could increase my booking rate and I'm a $10 million company by 1% that's $100,000 for the company, right?" (S006)

- **Changed emphasis:** 2021-10 he ranked conversion above all: "Conversion rate is more important than anything. Why would I advertise to just let jobs go?" (S011) From 2023 on (newer) booking rate is the first fix (S014, S006, S016). Both are inferred from S011, S014, S006, S016 to sit in one funnel, not to conflict.

## Technician pay and performance pay
- **Stance:** Pay on performance, not hours: a percentage of ticket less parts and tax, or sold hours, tiered by level and driven by a scorecard. Build it from the profit you need and around your top performer. Sources: S003, S004, S007, S008, S009, S010, S013, S014.
- 2023-03: "I hate the word commission. I use the word performance pay." (S004)
- 2023-05: "Performance pay was a miracle." (S010) "I figure out pay structures based on where I want my EBITDA to be." (S010)
- 2023-08: "I take the total ticket minus the parts cost and the tax. And then I pay a percentage." (S008) "You don't want to screw with their pay very often." (S008)
- 2024-02: "I find it hard to pay people extra to do their core job." (S007)
- 2024-05: "You build the process around your top guy." (S009) Hourly pay fails: "The more I put in, the less I get out." (S009)

- **Scorecards:** Four to six metrics per role, graded against the mean, average ticket weighted most for techs, booking rate for CSRs. Scorecards drive pay, promotion, and one-on-ones. Sources: S004, S008, S010, S015.
- 2023-08: "Every role, there should be four or five, maybe six things, tops." (S008) "We grade everything against the mean." (S008)
- 2026-02: "And the scorecards turn into the one on ones." (S015)
