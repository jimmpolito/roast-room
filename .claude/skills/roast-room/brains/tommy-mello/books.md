# Books: Tommy Mello

Built from the four batches of notes on S001 to S016. Two books of his own, then the books he recommends. Book text was not fetched; summaries are in my own words from how he describes them. Publication years are not stated in the corpus, so none are given.

## His own books

### Home Service Millionaire
- **Core thesis:** His story of going from about $50,000 in debt to a $30 million company in seven years, and the case that a trade business only scales on systems, pricing, and people rather than the owner's labor.
- **Key frameworks:** creative justification (owners explaining away why their numbers are bad, his term in S014); systems you can duplicate; charge enough to fund a real business.
- **Taught in sources:** S002 (summary of the book), S007 (referenced), S014 (named as his first book and the source of creative justification).

### Elevate
- **Core thesis:** How to attract, retain, and develop A players, and how he built the team behind A1.
- **Key frameworks:** recruiting where the avatar hangs out; trial-period hiring funnel; one A player worth five B players; the equity incentive program (he says the program is in the book, S008); scorecards and performance pay.
- **Taught in sources:** S002, S007, S008, S010, S012, S013, S014. Note: the foreword read aloud in S004 was written by Tom Howard, not Tommy.

### Planned or partial
- Books on performance pay and equity incentives (working title "Pay Them What They're Worth") were planned as of 2024 (S009). An ADHD book for kids is paused because his team did not think it was good enough (S012). He wrote one chapter, on treating employees as internal customers, in a multi-author book, The CVC Success Journal (S011).

## Books he recommends, and what he takes from them

He says he skims and marks books and uses audio: "I'm definitely not cover to cover." (S005) And he favors rereading a few: "You don't need to read 500 books. You need to read five books a 100 times." (S015)

- **The Seven Power Contractor (Al Levi):** the base of his manuals, SOPs, checklists, and delegation steps; he paid Al to tear up his business and build systems. Sources: S003, S004, S006, S007, S009, S012, S015.
- **Influence (Robert Cialdini):** reciprocity, active listening, and showing value in the sales process; he calls it the best book you will ever read. Sources: S002, S003, S005, S013, S014, S015.
- **How to Win Friends and Influence People (Dale Carnegie):** sales as relationships and listening; what he would read instead of a degree. Sources: S002, S006, S008, S012, S013, S014.
- **Dan Kennedy (No BS Direct Response Marketing, No BS Marketing to the Affluent):** marketing must make the phone ring; buy brand and direct response before paid media; aim at premium buyers who look in different places. Sources: S004, S006, S008, S009, S016.
- **The E-Myth (Michael Gerber):** a CPA's gift in 2007; the business as a system you can duplicate. Sources: S003, S012, S015, S016.
- **$100M Offers (Alex Hormozi):** find the starving crowd before polishing the offer, charge high, and sell speed, since people pay for speed over cost; he links it to Dan Kennedy on discounting drawing the worst customers. Sources: S002, S003.
- **Rocket Fuel and Traction (Gino Wickman):** visionary versus integrator (he puts himself far on the visionary side); EOS as a starting point for a company with no systems. Sources: S003, S004, S005, S006, S009.
- **What Should We Do? (Joe Crisara):** packages and options in home service sales. Sources: S006, S009, S012.
- **The One Thing (Gary Keller):** focus on one thing before spreading out; the story that moved him from many income streams to one. Sources: S009, S015.
- **The Ultimate Sales Machine (Chet Holmes):** stay top of mind with small, thoughtful gifts to the clients you want. Sources: S014, S015.
- **Patrick Lencioni (The Five Dysfunctions of a Team, The Ideal Team Player, The Motive):** departments putting themselves ahead of the company; his starter reading for a leadership team. Sources: S006, S009, S013.
- **Who (Geoff Smart) and Who Not How / 10X Is Easier Than 2X (Dan Sullivan, Benjamin Hardy):** his hiring resource; do your superpower and find the person who has done it before. Sources: S003, S004, S012, S014.
- **Go for No:** given to every technician; aim for the no. Sources: S003, S012.
- **Branded Not Blended (Dan Antonelli), StoryBrand:** brand consistency and creative. Sources: S006, S012.
- **Buy Back Your Time (Dan Martell):** time audit, hiring a driver and chef, success guides recorded on video. Source: S016.
- **How to Double Your Business in Six Months or Less (Bob Pfeiffer):** buying and fixing an unprofitable business, since you cannot cut your way to the top. Source: S009.
- **A Stake in the Outcome:** performance pay and profit sharing. Source: S014.
- **Acres of Diamonds:** a business owes its people and customers a profit. Source: S010.

Also named without much argument: Think and Grow Rich, The Compound Effect, Profit First, The Sales Boss, The Coaching Effect, Never Split the Difference, Raving Fans, Built to Last, To Sell Is Human, The Infinite Game, Two-Second Lean, Meetings Suck, Unchained, The Referral Engine (S002, S003, S004, S005, S008, S011, S012, S013, S015, S016).
