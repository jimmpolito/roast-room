# Positions: Mel Robbins

34 topics, built from the four notes batches over S001 to S016 (podcast episodes, 2022-10 to 2026-06). Each stance has a verbatim quote of Mel's own, a source id and the episode date. Guest views (S002, S006, S007, S008, S010, S012, S013, S015 are mostly guests talking) are not counted as hers unless she states them. Sponsor reads are left out. Most-used topics first. Topics with no evidence say "no recorded position".

## Acting before you feel ready
- **Stance:** Never wait for the feeling, the money, the timing or permission. Move first and the feeling follows. Thinking about the move is the trap.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "it's going to require you to take action and to take action before you feel ready." (S001) "The moment you have an instinct to act, you got to move within five seconds or else your brain will kill it." (S001)
  - 2022-12: "Action is the answer." (S014) "Today is the right day. And you're not starting over. You're starting with all of the experiences of your life." (S014)
  - 2023-12: "Knowing is not enough." (S003)
  - 2024-01: "Stop waiting to feel ready. Stop waiting for the money. Stop waiting for permission." (S011) "it's never going to feel like the right time. It's never going to feel perfect." (S011)
  - 2024-09: "You have to stop waiting for an invitation or a celebration and you have to create it." (S004)
- **Sources:** 7 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S006, S011, S014). Her most repeated stance.

## Motivation
- **Stance:** Motivation is unreliable and wears off, so build on action and small daily reps instead. Feeling flat is common, not a flaw.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "Motivation is garbage because it's never there when you need it." (S001) "Do not waste another day of your life waiting, wishing, or hoping motivation comes." (S001)
  - 2024-01: "That's why we quit because the newness is worn off." (S011)
  - 2024-09: "You're just not as motivated or as energized as you used to be." (S002)
- **Sources:** 4 (S001, S002, S006, S011).

## Fear
- **Stance:** Fear is the real blocker, not lack of instinct or skill, and it usually points at the right move. Expect it with anything new.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "it's so much easier to be angry and frustrated than it is to be afraid." (S001)
  - 2023-05: "The problem is never your instincts. The problem is your fear, and the fact that you're so scared of what everybody else thinks." (S005)
  - 2023-12: "understanding that fear is literally just a sign that you're about to try something." (S003)
  - 2024-02: "Nine times out of ten, I am afraid to do the thing that I know is right." (S008)
  - 2022-12: "That fear is going to disappear the second you start taking action." (S014)
- **Sources:** 6 (S001, S003, S005, S008, S010, S014).

## Excuses
- **Stance:** Every excuse is managed fear. "I don't know how", "too late" and "someone already did it" are excuses, and the answer is a small action, not another reason.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-12: "Any excuse that you have is just fear." (S014) "I'm too late. Somebody else already did it. I can't do it now." (S014)
  - 2024-02: "So not knowing how is an excuse to not figure out how to do it." (S008)
  - 2024-07: "I have been so sick of my excuses, but I had no idea what to do about it." (S006)
  - 2025-12: "And if Wallow can do this from a prison cell, you can do this from wherever you are right now." (S012)
- **Sources:** 4 (S006, S008, S012, S014).

## Other people's opinions
- **Stance:** Fear of what others think drives procrastination, overthinking and perfectionism. Your decisions only have to make sense to you. Most of the judgment you fear is imagined.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2023-05: "it doesn't matter what anybody else thinks, because you're the one that's going to have to live with the decision." (S005)
  - 2025-09: "It makes you procrastinate. It makes you doubt yourself and question your worth. It can paralyze you with perfectionism. It's the reason why you overthink." (S009) "the only person your decisions need to make sense to is you." (S009)
  - 2025-12: "Most people hold themselves back not because of what other people are actually saying, but because of what you're saying yourself" (S012)
  - 2024-09: "There's a big difference between someone else thinking you're a good daughter or a good son, and you knowing that you're one." (S004)
- **Sources:** 7 (S003, S004, S005, S008, S009, S010, S012).

## Making a decision (and trusting your gut)
- **Stance:** Your instincts are fine; what you lack is courage. Step away from the crowd, check whether the option expands or shrinks you, picture yourself a year out, and decide from pride, not guilt.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "you're one decision away from a different life. Do not wait around for the motivation to make that decision." (S001)
  - 2023-05: "stop saying trust your gut and start saying, I need to have the courage to make decisions" (S005) "There's a difference between something being easy and something being right." (S005)
  - 2024-02: "there is something more expansive. And there is something through that experience that I know is going to expand or grow or free me up." (S008)
  - 2024-09: "if you don't want to be controlled by guilt, just act in a way that makes you proud of yourself." (S004)
- **Sources:** 5 (S001, S004, S005, S008, S009).

## Overthinking and procrastination
- **Stance:** Procrastination is a stress response, not laziness. Busy-work is a founder's form of it. Planning and comparing can be self-doubt in disguise.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "because thinking about this tool is not going to change your life." (S001)
  - 2023-12: "it is triggered by stress." (S003) "there is a form of what I call business procrastination, where you are busy doing everything, but your business, right?" (S003)
  - 2024-01: "And I'm here to tell you right now that today is that someday." (S011)
  - 2024-09: "You're not gonna see the result right away. You're wired to move away from the pain." (S002)
- **Sources:** 6 (S001, S002, S003, S007, S009, S011).

## Confidence
- **Stance:** Confidence is a skill built under pressure, not a feeling you wait for. Skill and practice come first; the feeling catches up.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "You are building a muscle, a muscle of courage, of confidence, of action." (S001)
  - 2023-12: "Confidence is the willingness to try." (S003) "you build the skills, which lowers the resistance and fear, which makes you competent, which then makes you catch up to feeling confident." (S003)
  - 2024-01: "Confidence is not something you build when you're winning." (S011) "It is a skill that you build, it is a habit that you keep." (S011)
- **Sources:** 4 (S001, S003, S007, S011).

## Self-doubt and imposter feelings
- **Stance:** Self-doubt is the main obstacle and hides behind overthinking, people-pleasing and perfectionism. Feeling like an imposter means you are a beginner mid-bridge, not a fraud. Staying in doubt is a choice.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2023-12: "The single biggest obstacle that is in your way is the self-doubt and the internal critic." (S003) "imposter syndrome only means you're on the bridge." (S003)
  - 2024-01: "you're not afraid, you're not an imposter, you're not unworthy. You're just a beginner." (S011)
  - 2026-05: "self-doubt shows up as lots of different things, overthinking, people-pleasing, perfectionism, procrastination, comparison, blame, resentment" (S007)
  - 2025-12: "There is nothing holding you back except for you." (S012)
- **Sources:** 5 (S003, S007, S008, S011, S012).

## Quitting a job to start a business
- **Stance:** Want it, but do the numbers. Keep the day job while the business grows, study cash flow, cut spending to extend runway, and make a calculated decision rather than a leap. Expect the people close to you to call it reckless.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2023-12: "too many people make the leap instead of making the decision." (S003) "You have cut back on your spending in order to give yourself a bigger runway." (S003) She cites research that those who kept day jobs "were 33% less likely to fail in their new venture." (S003)
  - 2025-09: "when you go into business for yourself and you quit that job, that sounds reckless." (S009) "two years from now, everybody that questioned your decision to start that business is going to be bragging about your new business." (S009)
  - 2025-12: saying the want out loud is not a commitment: "Doesn't mean you have to end the marriage. Doesn't mean you have to quit the job. Doesn't mean you have to do anything." (S010)
- **Read:** the push to act is paired with a money check here; she separates the decision (now) from the leap (when the numbers allow). Inferred from S003, S009, S010.
- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S009, S010).

## Quitting versus persisting
- **Stance:** Quitting is the only way to lose. Expect a dip around day 19 of anything new, treat a missed day as a bump, and go back to why you started. She does not give a rule for when to stop a business; her test is whether it is a hard no or a knowing to keep going.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2024-01: "If you know the day that people are most likely to quit, you can take the steps to avoid it." (S011) "I just had a day. That's it. Just had a day." (S011) "Just because it's hard, doesn't mean you can't do it." (S011)
  - 2024-02: "is it a no like hell no, we're not doing this anymore? Or is it a knowing to keep going?" (S008)
  - 2025-12: "as long as you don't quit, you can't lose." (S010)
- **Sources:** 3 (S008, S010, S011).

## Burnout and work hours
- **Stance (newest, her own practice):** Overwork is a business mistake, not a badge, but she admits she keeps relapsing into it.
- **Over time:**
  - 2014 to about 2020: she worked hundred-hour weeks and called it success, until nervous-system research in the pandemic made her stop (S003, her account in 2023).
  - 2022-12: "I don't think I ever got out of the mode of relating to work as though I was in an emergency." (S014) "I can see that I've been addicted to being busy for a long time." (S014)
  - 2023-12 (talk to entrepreneurs): "Wellness and self-care is a business strategy." (S003) "if you're working all the time, you're not working smart." (S003) On hustle culture: "which might work for single guys, but it does not work for anybody with a family." (S003)
  - 2023-12 (three weeks later, about her own year): "I worked way too much this year." (S016) "to work like 90 hours a week for an entire year, especially when I had made a commitment to heal and be happy." (S016)
  - 2026-06: she opens on her audience's burnout: "You're too tired to fight for what you want at work. And you're counting yourself out before you even try." (S015)
- **Read:** she preaches breaks ahead of her own practice and says so. Inferred from S003, S014, S016.
- **Sources:** 4 (S003, S014, S015, S016).

## Balance versus boundaries (changed view)
- **Earlier (before 2023):** she chased balance and it made her miserable (S016).
- **Newer, 2023-12:** "Last year was the year that I finally realized, Mel, screw balance." (S016) "balance is something you wish for. Boundaries are what you need to create." (S016) "People are not mind readers. Boundaries only work if you're willing to communicate them." (S016)
- **Sources:** 1 (S016).

## Debt
- **Stance:** Nobody is coming to save you. Open the bills, outwork the hole, and once out, protect yourself with savings. She treats putting the house up as collateral as a common small-business trap, from experience, not as a rule.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: the $800,000 hole is her origin story for acting without motivation (S001).
  - 2024-03: "Like that literally is the answer that I realized nobody was coming." (S013) "Like a lot of you who are small business owners, we put up our house as the collateral for that business because that's all the collateral that we had." (S013) "If you think a credit card bill is scary, that is nothing compared to what it feels like to have leans start arriving in the mail" (S013)
- **Read:** her exit was working several jobs, and later episodes treat that same emergency pace as the thing that burned her out. Inferred from S013, S014, S016.
- **Sources:** 4 (S001, S011, S013, S014).

## Money habits and saving
- **Stance:** Use a hard rule at the source instead of willpower. She is candid that she earns well but manages money badly, so her money advice is thin.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2024-03: "Any dollar that came in, 50 cents had to go into savings." (S013) "I am unbelievably talented when it comes to making money, but I am not a financial expert." (S013) "I shop when I'm stressed and even though I can pay my bills at this moment in my life" (S013) "I probably have too much in terms of savings, but it's just sitting somewhere where the market can't affect it" (S013)
  - 2025-09: do not spend to avoid disappointing people: "why are you giving away your money so that people are not disappointed or bummed out with you?" (S009)
- **Note:** the 70/30 rule and budget steps in S013 are the guest's (Tiffany Aliche); Mel only restates them.
- **Sources:** 2 (S009, S013).

## Saying yes under money pressure
- **Stance:** Scarcity makes you say yes to everything; know that is happening. "you will say yes to anything that you need to say yes to to stay afloat." (S014, 2022-12) Before agreeing, ask why: "Do it for you, don't do it for them." (S009, 2025-09)
- **Sources:** 2 (S009, S014).

## Pricing and discounting
- **Stance:** Do not underprice to friends or discount out of fear that people will not see your value. Charge for a service, including friends.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2025-09: lists underpricing as self-devaluing: "If you're a business owner and You underpriced your products, or you give your services at a lower value" (S009)
  - 2024-03: "Do not give that shit to your friends for free because you will start to resent them" (S013) (said in agreement with the guest).
- **Gap:** no recorded position on how to set a price, raise prices, or discount as a sales tactic.
- **Sources:** 2 (S009, S013).

## Partners and cofounders
- **Stance:** A partner helps against loneliness when starting from zero, but never bring one in to fix problems you have not diagnosed. Hire the expertise first and see if a partnership grows from it.
- **Evidence (2023-12):** "If you're starting a business, it's great to start with a partner because it's not as lonely." (S003) "do not make the mistake of thinking that a partner is going to solve all your problems." (S003) "I had a partner who was fantastic and then that partner was really horrible." (S003)
- **Sources:** 1 (S003).

## Hiring and firing
- **Stance (thin):** For anything that creates friction, remove it, hire someone, or learn to do it without complaining (S006, 2024-07). Hire expertise before taking a partner (S003). On a slow business, ask whether you have too many people, the wrong ones, or whether the problem is you (S003).
- **Firing:** no recorded position of her own. Her husband laid off people they loved during the restaurant restructure (S001, story only).
- **Sources:** 2 (S003, S006).

## Focus versus diversification
- **Stance:** One focus at a time. Pick one thing a year, study it, then execute.
- **Evidence (2023-12):** "if everything is important, nothing is" (S003) "And so pick something for the year that you want to master and become a student of it. Then execute." (S003)
- **Sources:** 1 (S003), and her career moves fit it (inferred from S003, S006).

## A slow period: push through, pivot or close
- **Stance:** Treat a slump as a research project, not a verdict on your worth. Check project, process, product and people. Frustration means you have outgrown a system.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2023-12: "look at any slow period in your business as a research project." (S003) "So I want you to use the four P's, project, process, product, and people." (S003)
  - 2023-12: "Frustration is not a sign that things are terrible. Frustration is a sign that you're growing." (S016) "It's not about you, it's about the systems." (S016)
  - 2022-12: "Wherever you have friction, there is either a broken process, or there is something that is no longer aligned with you, or there are the wrong people around you." (S014)
- **Sources:** 3 (S003, S014, S016).

## Being stuck
- **Stance:** Stuck is a signal, not a verdict. Write down the friction, make each item a project, set a goal in the future (any goal), and take a tiny daily step. Most people know what they want and have talked themselves out of it.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2024-07: "everything on that list is now a project." (S006) "if you turn it into an experiment, this is no longer about getting it right, it's about getting yourself moving." (S006)
  - 2025-12: "I don't believe people don't know what they want. I believe you know deeply what you want, but you have convinced yourself it's not possible" (S010) "I've become convinced, Hoda, convinced that the number one thing that stands in everybody's way is discouragement." (S010)
- **Sources:** 4 (S006, S010, S014, S016).

## Timing and age
- **Stance:** You are never too late or too early. "Too old" and "too young" are stories that action disproves. She launched the podcast at 56.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-12: "You're not too late. You're not too early." (S014)
  - 2024-01: "because time keeps going even if you don't." (S011)
  - 2024-09: "It's complete fiction. The action is actually the only way that you prove that that crap in your mind is a lie." (S002) "years go by as you're waiting for the right time to happen or you're waiting for the celebration." (S004)
- **Sources:** 4 (S002, S004, S011, S014).

## Failure
- **Stance:** You will probably fail; what counts is what you do next. Each failure equips you for the next thing.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "are you going to fail? Probably." (S001) "because it's what you do after you fail." (S001)
  - 2024-01: "every single failure has honest to God equipped me with the lessons and the skill or the wisdom that I needed to be able to do something even cooler down the line." (S011) "I've failed more times than I have time to tell you." (S011)
- **Sources:** 2 (S001, S011).

## Habits, consistency and how long change takes
- **Stance:** Small daily reps (5 to 15 minutes) over bursts; fix habits as systems, not character; give a big change a year.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2022-10: "And look, it doesn't work overnight. It works over time." (S001)
  - 2023-12: "Give yourself a year because your whole life can change in a year." (S016) "Habits are just systems and processes in your life that you repeat." (S016)
  - 2024-01: "You can find 5, 10, 15 minutes a day to start chipping away" (S011)
  - 2024-07: "I would wake up every day and I would take 15 minutes of action a day." (S006)
- **Sources:** 4 (S001, S006, S011, S016).

## Responsibility for other people (people-pleasing)
- **Stance:** You are not responsible for other people's happiness, rescuing them, making them understand your choices, or proving your worth. Let them be disappointed.
- **Evidence:**
  - 2025-09: "Let them be unhappy. Let them be disappointed." (S009) "I think you've confused caring about people with carrying their burden." (S009) "Save your energy for living your life, not defending it." (S009)
  - 2024-09: "They don't need to be managed. They need to be accepted as they are." (S004)
- **Sources:** 2 (S004, S009).

## Time management and the workday
- **Stance:** Your attention is the scarcest resource. Protect the first part of the morning, cut news and low-value input, take 15 minutes on what matters before email.
- **Evidence (2023-12):** "The most important resource that you have as a business owner is your time, your attention, and your energy." (S003) "And zone one, in my opinion, is the only time that you have that is yours. And you have to fight for it." (S003) "whatever it is that you allow as input profoundly impacts your output." (S003)
- **Sources:** 1 (S003), with the time-and-energy idea echoed in S011 (2024-01).

## Marketing, PR and content
- **Stance:** Do not spend on PR. Your customers and your own social media are your PR.
- **Evidence (2023-12):** "Your customers are your single biggest PR and your social media." (S003)
- **Gap:** no recorded position on paid ads, niche, or brand strategy.
- **Sources:** 1 (S003).

## Competition and peers
- **Stance:** Competitors are not your enemy; join communities of other operators, because family and friends will not understand a founder's life.
- **Evidence (2023-12):** "the mistake that we always make is we think that our competitors are against us." (S003) "Your family has no idea what you're going through. Your friends have no idea what you're going through." (S003)
- **Sources:** 1 (S003).

## Visibility, pay and promotion at work
- **Stance:** Do not assume your work will be noticed; make it known, and bring a better way rather than a complaint. Mostly the guest's framework in S015; Mel's own line is a regret.
- **Evidence (2026-06):** "I think I made the mistake in my career early of just assuming that somebody else would notice that." (S015) "You have power. Use it." (S015)
- **Sources:** 1 (S015).

## AI
- **Stance (2026-06, only source):** Use it now despite its flaws; it gives tired people time back, and those who skip it fall behind.
- **Evidence:** "I see it as this incredible resource, if you learn to use it, to give you time back." (S015) "You will be if you let this pass you by." (S015) "you better jump on the dance floor." (S015)
- **Sources:** 1 (S015).
