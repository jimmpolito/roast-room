# Principles: Mel Robbins

Built from the four notes batches on 16 podcast episodes (S001 to S016, 2022-10 to 2026-06). 26 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" is the number of distinct episodes that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Guest episodes count only where Mel says it in her own words.

1. **Do not wait for motivation or readiness; act first and the feeling follows.** "Motivation is garbage because it's never there when you need it." (S001) "Stop waiting to feel ready. Stop waiting for the money. Stop waiting for permission." (S011) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S003, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

2. **Every excuse is fear in disguise; answer it with a small action, not a better reason.** "Any excuse that you have is just fear." (S014) "So not knowing how is an excuse to not figure out how to do it." (S008) Sources: 4 (S008, S010, S012, S014). Confidence: high.

3. **Fear usually points at the right move.** "Nine times out of ten, I am afraid to do the thing that I know is right." (S008) "understanding that fear is literally just a sign that you're about to try something." (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S005, S008). Confidence: high.

4. **The main thing in the way is you, not other people or circumstances.** "the fact is you're the one in your own way." (S012) "The single biggest obstacle that is in your way is the self-doubt and the internal critic." (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S010, S012). Confidence: high.

5. **A decision only has to make sense to the person who lives with it, so stop polling people.** "the only person your decisions need to make sense to is you." (S009) "it doesn't matter what anybody else thinks, because you're the one that's going to have to live with the decision." (S005) Sources: 3 (S004, S005, S009). Confidence: high.

6. **Today is the right day; waiting for the right time costs years.** "Today is the right day. And you're not starting over. You're starting with all of the experiences of your life." (S014) "And I'm here to tell you right now that today is that someday." (S011) Sources: 3 (S004, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

7. **Small daily action on the big thing beats bursts and grand plans.** "You can find 5, 10, 15 minutes a day to start chipping away" (S011) "I made a decision that I was going to start working on it." (S006) Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S006, S011). Confidence: high.

8. **Confidence comes from doing the thing, not before it.** "Confidence is the willingness to try." (S003) "Confidence is not something you build when you're winning." (S011) Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S007, S011). Confidence: high.

9. **Feeling like an imposter on something new means you are a beginner, not unqualified.** "you're not afraid, you're not an imposter, you're not unworthy. You're just a beginner." (S011) "imposter syndrome only means you're on the bridge." (S003) Sources: 4 (S003, S007, S011, S016). Confidence: high.

10. **Treat frustration and slow periods as a project with a cause, not a verdict on you.** "look at any slow period in your business as a research project." (S003) "So stop making it so personal." (S016) Sources: 4 (S003, S006, S014, S016). Confidence: high.

11. **Rest and boundaries are business strategy; overwork makes worse decisions.** "Wellness and self-care is a business strategy." (S003) "if you're working all the time, you're not working smart." (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S014, S016). Confidence: high. Changed view: she worked hundred-hour weeks from 2014 and called it success until the pandemic (S003, 2023-12); in 2022-12 she says "I can see that I've been addicted to being busy for a long time." (S014); in 2023-12 she still says she worked about 90 hours a week that year (S016). Newer stated view: boundaries over balance and hustle. Her practice lagged her advice.

12. **Before you leave a job, run the numbers; make a decision, not a leap.** "too many people make the leap instead of making the decision." (S003) "You have cut back on your spending in order to give yourself a bigger runway." (S003) Sources: 2 (S003, S013, where she tells how pledging the house for a business ended in $800K of debt). Confidence: medium.

13. **Expect the people closest to you not to understand a big change, and do not wait for them to.** "I want you to expect, as your friend, that they're not going to understand it because they're not doing it." (S009) "Your family has no idea what you're going through. Your friends have no idea what you're going through." (S003) Sources: 2 (S003, S009). Confidence: medium.

14. **Decide from pride, not guilt or fear of disappointing someone.** "Choose to do things because it makes you proud of yourself." (S004) "Do it for you, don't do it for them." (S009) Sources: 2 (S004, S009). Confidence: medium.

15. **Focus on the actions you control, not on what others will decide.** "You cannot control what other people are going to do, but you can truly wake up every day and just focus on the actions that you take, which are in your control." (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S009). Confidence: medium.

16. **Expect the dip and do not quit on a missed day.** "I just had a day. That's it. Just had a day." (S011) "because it's what you do after you fail." (S001) Sources: 2 (S001, S011). Confidence: medium.

17. **Rejection is a count; quitting is the only way to lose.** "as long as you don't quit, you can't lose." (S010) "Winter's day is when a lot of us keep going." (S011) Sources: 2 (S010, S011). Confidence: medium.

18. **Know why you want it; an idea alone will not carry you.** "Just having a business idea isn't enough." (S003) "Why did you want to do this?" (S011) Sources: 2 (S003, S011). Confidence: medium.

19. **Pick one focus, because you cannot push everything at once.** "if everything is important, nothing is" (S003) "you need to start to direct your time and energy at the things that you want, not at the things that you have." (S011) Sources: 2 (S003, S011). Confidence: medium.

20. **Charge for your work, including to friends; underpricing out of fear breeds resentment.** "Do not give that shit to your friends for free because you will start to resent them" (S013) "If you're a business owner and You underpriced your products, or you give your services at a lower value" (S009) Sources: 2 (S009, S013). Confidence: medium.

21. **Fix the system, not your character.** "It's not about you, it's about the systems." (S016) "you're going to hear her say, you don't need discipline. You just need to understand these tricks." (S013) Sources: 2 (S013, S016). Confidence: medium.

22. **Make your value known; nobody notices it for you.** "I think I made the mistake in my career early of just assuming that somebody else would notice that." (S015) "and you can then present it and do it, not talk about it, do it. You are indispensable." (S015) Sources: 1 (S015, a guest episode; these lines are Mel's). Confidence: low.

23. **Big change takes a year, and you have to slow down to make it.** "Give yourself a year because your whole life can change in a year." (S016) "But you don't change your life when you're moving a million miles an hour." (S016) Sources: 1 (S016). Confidence: low.

24. **Do not bring in a partner to fix problems you have not diagnosed; hire the expertise first.** "do not make the mistake of thinking that a partner is going to solve all your problems." (S003) Sources: 1 (S003). Confidence: low.

25. **Spend on customers and your own channels, not PR, and treat competitors as peers.** "Your customers are your single biggest PR and your social media." (S003) "the mistake that we always make is we think that our competitors are against us." (S003) Sources: 1 (S003). Confidence: low.

26. **Start using new tools like AI now, flaws and all, or get left behind.** "you better jump on the dance floor." (S015) "I see it as this incredible resource, if you learn to use it, to give you time back." (S015) Sources: 1 (S015). Confidence: low.

Inconsistency to keep in view (inferred from S001, S003, S013): her self-made recovery story is to outwork a crisis ("Like that literally is the answer that I realized nobody was coming." (S013)), while her later rule is that overwork hurts decisions. Both are hers; the newer view (2023 onward) is rest as strategy.
